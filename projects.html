Slide-Level Active Learning for H&E Histopathology

Self-Supervised Learning for Stimulated Raman Histology

Developed SimCLR and contrastive-learning representations for stimulated Raman histology, enabling brain metastasis classification in limited-annotation settings.

Uncertainty-Guided PET/CT Tumor Segmentation

At the University of Geneva, combined Monte Carlo Dropout, SimCLR, and 3D U-Net for active learning in PET/CT tumor segmentation, reducing annotation effort by 60% while maintaining near fully supervised accuracy.

PET/CT3D U-NetMC Dropout
