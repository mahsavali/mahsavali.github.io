Mahsa Vali

PhD Candidate in Computer Science · University of Cologne

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Cologne, working at the intersection of annotation-efficient and trustworthy AI for medical image analysis. My research focuses on active learning, self-supervised learning, uncertainty quantification, foundation models, and vision-language models for computational pathology and multimodal medical imaging.

I develop reproducible PyTorch pipelines and scalable training workflows for HPC/SLURM environments, with applications spanning H&E histopathology, stimulated Raman histology, PET/CT, retinal imaging, and multimodal medical data.

Research Snapshot

H&E Active Learning

Slide-level uncertainty-guided active learning for multi-class histopathology segmentation, validated across five external cohorts.

PET/CT Segmentation

Uncertainty-guided active learning using Monte Carlo Dropout, SimCLR, and 3D U-Net, reducing annotation effort by 60% while maintaining near fully supervised accuracy.

Pathology Vision-Language Models

Analysis of hallucination, EOS prediction, and autoregressive decoding in BioGPT and HistoGPT, including decoding-level mitigation strategies.
