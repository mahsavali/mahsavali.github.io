Medical AI · Computer Vision · Computational Pathology

Mahsa Vali

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Cologne, working at the intersection of annotation-efficient and trustworthy AI for medical image analysis.

My research focuses on active learning, self-supervised learning, uncertainty quantification, foundation models, and vision-language models across computational pathology and multimodal medical imaging, including H&E, PET/CT, and retinal imaging. I build reproducible PyTorch pipelines and large-scale training workflows for HPC/SLURM environments.
